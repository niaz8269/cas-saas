# Site images — drop your photos here

Put your own (or properly licensed) photos in this folder and they appear on the
public site automatically. Until a file exists, the site shows a designed navy+gold
gradient in its place, so nothing ever looks broken.

**Do not use competitors' or unlicensed photos** — it's a copyright risk and Google
penalizes duplicate/stolen imagery. Use your own campus/student/office photos, or
royalty-free ones you're licensed to use (e.g. Unsplash/Pexels downloads).

## Files the site looks for

| File name (exact)      | Where it shows            | Recommended size |
|------------------------|---------------------------|------------------|
| `hero.jpg`             | Home hero background      | 1920 × 1080      |

## University covers & logos

University cover photos and logos are **not** set here — upload them per-university
in the admin panel (Catalog → University → edit), and they show on the university
cards and detail pages automatically.

## Tips
- Use `.jpg` for photos (smaller), `.png` for logos/graphics with transparency.
- Keep hero.jpg under ~400 KB (compress at tinypng.com) so the page loads fast — page speed is an SEO factor.
