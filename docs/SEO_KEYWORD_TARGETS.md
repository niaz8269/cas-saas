# SEO target searches (started 2026-11-06)

Source: Google's own search suggestions for the study-in-China topics (what people really type).
The two keyword-data tools connected to this project could not be used (Ahrefs: plan too low;
Semrush: out of API units), so there are no volume or difficulty numbers yet. Check these in
Search Console after two to four weeks, and fill in impressions and clicks per page.

The audience in the suggestions is mostly students from Pakistan, India and Africa, interested in
MBBS, scholarships, fees and visas.

## What is already done
- Pages with only a name, city and registered majors are kept out of search results and the sitemap
  until they have confirmed programs, application windows, a researched profile or MBBS approval.
  They switch on by themselves when any of those is added.
- Sitemaps, IndexNow pings, live counts in descriptions, city-link fix.
